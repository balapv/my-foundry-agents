## 1. Purpose

1.1 This guide defines when and how Tier 1 Customer Care, including the Contoso Air digital assistant, escalates a customer contact, which team receives each type of escalation, the authority limits of each tier and what the customer should be told.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This guide applies to all Customer Care channels: telephone, chat, messaging, email and the Contoso Air digital assistant. It applies together with the Flight Disruption and Rebooking Policy (CA-CC-POL-014), the Meal and Hotel Assistance Policy (CA-CC-POL-021) and Contoso Air Altitude Loyalty Benefits (CA-LOY-POL-003).

## 3. Definitions

3.1 Tier 1 Customer Care: frontline agents and the Contoso Air digital assistant.

3.2 Tier 2 Customer Care: Customer Care Specialists and Supervisors who handle escalated cases.

3.3 Specialist desks: the Irregular Operations (IROPS) Recovery Desk, Accessibility Services, the Unaccompanied Minor Desk and the International Travel Documentation Desk.

3.4 Reservation or traveling party: all passengers booked on the same reservation (confirmation code). The primary traveler is the passenger who holds or made the reservation.

3.5 Handoff summary: the written summary that accompanies every escalation, as described in Section 8.

## 4. Escalation Principles

4.1 Never fabricate information. If information cannot be found or confirmed, say so.

4.2 Never confirm an action that has not been completed. A rebooking, refund request or escalation may be described as done only after the system confirms it.

4.3 Confirm before changing reservations. The customer must explicitly agree to a proposed change before it is made.

4.4 Verify identity under the Customer Authentication Standard (CA-CC-STD-002) before discussing or changing a reservation.

4.5 Never promise or imply the outcome of an escalation.

4.6 Disclose reservation details only to the primary traveler or to a passenger on the reservation who has been verified under CA-CC-STD-002.

4.7 Record every escalation in the case record, including the receiving team, the time of escalation and the handoff summary.

## 6. Escalation Matrix

| Situation | Escalate to | Target response time | What to tell the customer |
|---|---|---|---|
| No satisfactory same-day alternatives | IROPS Recovery Desk | 30 minutes | The IROPS Recovery Desk will review further options; no new itinerary is confirmed until it is completed. |
| Splitting a traveling party | Tier 2 Customer Care | 15 minutes | A Specialist will discuss the options; a child under 15 will never be separated from an accompanying adult. |
| Accessibility requirements | Accessibility Services | 15 minutes | An Accessibility Services specialist will confirm the arrangements. |
| Special circumstances | Tier 2 Customer Care | 15 minutes | A Specialist will review your circumstances and the options available. |
| Unaccompanied minors | Unaccompanied Minor Desk | Immediate transfer | The Unaccompanied Minor Desk must approve any change for the minor. |
| International documentation concerns | International Travel Documentation Desk | 2 hours | A documentation specialist will review the requirements. |
| Irregular operations | IROPS Recovery Desk | 30 minutes | The IROPS Recovery Desk is handling the disruption and will contact the customer. |
| Conflicting reservation records | IROPS Recovery Desk | 30 minutes | The flight status cannot be confirmed yet; please monitor notifications. |
| Requests outside agent authority | Tier 2 Customer Care | 24 hours | The request has been referred; no outcome can be promised. |
| System or tool unavailability | Retry, then Tier 2 Customer Care | Retry after 15 minutes | The information cannot currently be confirmed. |

## 7. Situation Procedures

### 7.1 No Satisfactory Same-Day Alternatives

7.1.1 When no same-day eligible alternative accommodates the whole traveling party, or the customer does not accept the available alternatives, Tier 1 must explain the earliest eligible alternative that keeps the party together, including the next day, and offer escalation to the IROPS Recovery Desk.

7.1.2 Only the IROPS Recovery Desk may arrange reaccommodation on another airline or any option outside the eligible-alternative definition. Tier 1 must not promise these options.

### 7.2 Splitting Traveling Parties

7.2.1 Tier 1 must not split a traveling party. A split may be completed only by Tier 2 or the IROPS Recovery Desk, with the explicit consent of the primary traveler.

7.2.2 A child under 15 must never be separated from at least one accompanying adult on the same reservation, even with consent.

### 7.3 Accessibility Requirements

7.3.1 Any reaccommodation involving a wheelchair, mobility device, service animal or other accessibility requirement must be escalated to Accessibility Services at 1-800-555-0163 so that assistance is carried over to the new itinerary.

### 7.4 Unaccompanied Minors

7.4.1 Unaccompanied minors are children aged 5 to 14 traveling alone. Any change for an unaccompanied minor must be referred to the Unaccompanied Minor Desk at 1-800-555-0164.

7.4.2 An unaccompanied minor must never be rebooked onto a connecting itinerary or onto the last flight of the day without Unaccompanied Minor Desk approval.

### 7.5 International Documentation Concerns

7.5.1 Questions about passports, visas, transit permissions or entry requirements must be referred to the International Travel Documentation Desk at 1-800-555-0166. Tier 1 must not state whether a passenger's documents are sufficient.

### 7.6 Irregular Operations

7.6.1 During declared irregular operations, cases that Tier 1 cannot resolve under CA-CC-POL-014 are escalated to the IROPS Recovery Desk at 1-800-555-0161, available 24/7.

### 7.7 Conflicting Reservation Records

7.7.1 Records conflict when the reservation record explicitly records an operational outcome that the operational flight status contradicts, for example the reservation record shows a segment cancelled but operational status shows the flight operating. A segment status of Confirmed only means the customer holds a booked seat; it does not record whether the flight operates. A confirmed segment on a flight that operational status shows as cancelled is a normal cancellation, not a conflict, and is handled under CA-CC-POL-014. When records conflict, Tier 1 must:

- treat the information as unconfirmed;
- not tell the customer that the flight is operating or cancelled as fact;
- escalate to the IROPS Recovery Desk for verification; and
- advise the customer to monitor flight notifications.

### 7.8 Customer Requests Outside Agent Authority

7.8.1 Requests for compensation, cash, free upgrades, fee waivers beyond policy or travel credits are outside Tier 1 authority. Tier 1 must not promise or imply approval and must explain the applicable policy respectfully.

7.8.2 Only Tier 2 Specialists may issue goodwill travel credits, up to US$200 per passenger per incident. Amounts above US$200 require Customer Care Manager approval.

7.8.3 Upgrades are never offered as compensation. No cash compensation is provided for domestic cancellations or delays beyond refunds and the assistance in CA-CC-POL-021.

7.8.4 A suitable response is: "I understand why you are asking. Under our policy I am not able to approve that, but I can refer your request to a Customer Care Specialist, who will review it. I cannot promise the outcome."

7.8.5 Every goodwill travel credit must be recorded in the case record with the reason, the amount and the approver.

### 7.9 System or Tool Unavailability

7.9.1 When reservation or operational systems cannot be reached, Customer Care must not guess. Customer Care must tell the customer that the information cannot currently be confirmed and offer to retry or escalate. If the system remains unavailable after 30 minutes, the case is escalated to Tier 2.

### 7.10 Special Circumstances

7.10.1 Special circumstances include a medical condition affecting travel, a bereavement, travel with an infant requiring specific arrangements and a passenger at risk of being stranded. Tier 1 must acknowledge the circumstance, must not request sensitive details beyond what is needed to route the case and must escalate to Tier 2 Customer Care, or to Accessibility Services where accessibility needs are involved. Special circumstances do not by themselves create entitlements beyond CA-CC-POL-014 and CA-CC-POL-021.

## 8. Digital Assistant Handoff Procedure

8.1 When the digital assistant escalates, it must transfer a handoff summary so that the customer does not have to repeat themselves. The summary must include:

- the issue, in one or two sentences;
- what has been verified, including identity, confirmation code and flight status;
- what the customer wants; and
- actions already taken and options already offered or declined.

8.2 The digital assistant must tell the customer which team will handle the request, the target response time from Section 6 and that no outcome is confirmed until that team completes it.

8.3 If a live transfer is not available, the digital assistant must provide the relevant contact number from Section 5.

## 9. Authority Limits

| Action | Tier 1 (agents and digital assistant) | Tier 2 Specialists and Supervisors |
|---|---|---|
| Rebook onto an eligible alternative | Yes, with customer agreement | Yes |
| Waive change fee and fare difference for carrier disruptions | Yes, per CA-CC-POL-014 | Yes |
| Split a traveling party | No | Yes, with primary traveler consent |
| Rebook on another airline | No, IROPS Recovery Desk only | No, IROPS Recovery Desk only |
| Lower cabin with refund of fare difference | Yes, with customer consent | Yes |
| Submit a refund request | Yes | Yes |
| Issue meal or hotel vouchers | No | No |
| Issue goodwill travel credit | No | Up to US$200 per passenger per incident |
| Cash compensation or complimentary upgrades | No | No |
| Fee waivers beyond policy | No | No, Customer Care Manager only |

## 10. De-escalation

10.1 Acknowledge the disruption and its impact before explaining policy.

10.2 Use clear language. Explain what can be done, what cannot be done and why.

10.3 Offer choices where policy allows and let the customer decide.

10.4 Do not argue, blame other teams or speculate about causes.

10.5 If the customer remains dissatisfied, offer escalation under Section 6.

10.6 If a customer becomes abusive or threatening, Customer Care may end the contact after one warning and must record the reason. Threats to safety at an airport must be reported to airport customer service immediately.

## 11. Exceptions

11.1 Exceptions to this guide may be approved only by the Senior Manager, Customer Care Operations, or a Customer Care Manager, and must be recorded in the case record. Amounts and exceptions above Tier 2 authority require Customer Care Manager approval.
