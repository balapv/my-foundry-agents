## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This policy defines when Contoso Air provides meal vouchers, hotel accommodation and ground transportation to passengers whose travel is disrupted, how that assistance is issued and how passengers are reimbursed when vouchers are not available.

1.2 This policy applies to all Customer Care channels, including Tier 1 Customer Care (frontline agents and the Contoso Air digital assistant), Tier 2 Customer Care Specialists and Supervisors, the IROPS Recovery Desk and airport customer service. It applies to passengers holding confirmed tickets on Contoso Air-operated flights.

1.3 Rebooking, change-fee waivers and refunds are governed by the Flight Disruption and Rebooking Policy (CA-CC-POL-014). This policy does not provide cash compensation.

1.4 The objective of this policy is to ensure that eligible passengers receive consistent, timely assistance at every airport, and that Customer Care does not promise assistance before eligibility has been confirmed.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 Carrier-initiated cancellation: a Contoso Air flight cancelled by Contoso Air for any reason.

2.2 Controllable disruption: a cancellation or delay caused by circumstances within Contoso Air's control, including crew availability, aircraft maintenance, operational disruption, IT systems and aircraft swaps.

2.3 Uncontrollable disruption: a cancellation or delay caused by circumstances outside Contoso Air's control, including weather, air traffic control, airport closures, security events and government orders.

2.4 Significant delay: a departure delay of 3 hours or more versus scheduled departure.

2.5 Reservation or traveling party: all passengers booked on the same reservation (confirmation code).

2.6 Eligible alternative: a Contoso Air-operated flight between the same origin and destination, departing on the original travel date or the following calendar day, nonstop or with at most one connection, with confirmed seat availability.

2.7 Meal period: breakfast (5:00 AM to 10:59 AM), lunch (11:00 AM to 4:59 PM) or dinner (5:00 PM to 10:59 PM), in local time at the airport where the passenger is waiting.

2.8 Home airport metropolitan area: the metropolitan area of the passenger's city of residence, as identified in the customer profile, or the origin of the reservation when the trip begins at home.

## 3. Qualifying Controllable Disruptions

3.1 Meal and hotel assistance applies only to controllable disruptions. A carrier-initiated cancellation or delay qualifies only when the disruption reason code recorded in the operational system is controllable.

3.2 Uncontrollable disruptions, including weather, air traffic control, airport closures, security events and government orders, do not qualify for complimentary meals or hotels. In these cases Customer Care may share discounted distressed passenger hotel rates available through Contoso Air partner hotels. The passenger books and pays for the room directly.

3.3 The disruption reason code recorded at the time assistance is requested determines eligibility. Customer Care must not speculate about the cause. If no reason code is recorded, Customer Care must tell the passenger that eligibility cannot yet be confirmed.

3.4 A missed connection on the same reservation qualifies only when the late arrival of the inbound Contoso Air-operated flight was caused by a controllable disruption.

3.5 If the recorded reason code is later corrected from uncontrollable to controllable, a passenger who met the conditions of Section 4 or Section 6 may request reimbursement of eligible expenses under Section 10. If the reason code is corrected from controllable to uncontrollable, vouchers already issued remain valid.

3.6 Loyalty status does not change eligibility under this policy. Contoso Air Altitude members at every tier receive the same meal and hotel assistance as other passengers.

## 4. Meal Eligibility

4.1 One meal voucher of US$15 is provided per passenger per meal period when a controllable disruption results in a wait at the airport of 3 hours or more beyond the original scheduled departure time.

4.2 No meal voucher is provided when the reaccommodated flight departs earlier than, or less than 3 hours after, the original scheduled departure time.

4.3 A passenger receives one voucher for each meal period that falls within the qualifying wait at the airport.

4.4 Meal vouchers are valid only on the date of issue, only at participating airport food outlets, and cannot be exchanged for cash. Unused value is not refunded.

4.5 Infants traveling on an adult's lap do not receive a separate meal voucher.

4.6 Example: if a controllable disruption moves a 2:00 PM departure to 6:30 PM, the wait is 4 hours 30 minutes and spans the lunch and dinner meal periods, so each passenger receives two US$15 vouchers. If the same flight is moved to 4:30 PM, the wait is less than 3 hours and no voucher is provided.

## 5. Overnight Disruption Rules

5.1 A controllable disruption results in an overnight stay when either of the following applies:

- the passenger is reaccommodated on a flight departing the next calendar day after the original scheduled departure; or
- the passenger is required to wait between 11:00 PM and 6:00 AM local time.

5.2 An overnight stay away from the home airport metropolitan area qualifies for hotel accommodation under Section 6.

5.3 An overnight stay within the home airport metropolitan area does not qualify for hotel accommodation. Section 7 applies instead.

5.4 A passenger who chooses to wait at the airport overnight instead of accepting an offered hotel is not entitled to reimbursement for a hotel later booked independently.

## 6. Hotel Eligibility

6.1 Hotel accommodation is provided only when a controllable disruption results in an overnight stay as defined in Section 5.1.

6.2 Hotel accommodation includes one standard room per reservation for up to 4 passengers. Reservations of 5 or more passengers receive 2 standard rooms. Additional rooms may be provided where required to meet a passenger's documented accessibility needs, as confirmed by Accessibility Services.

6.3 Hotel accommodation includes ground transportation to and from the hotel, by hotel shuttle or by a transportation voucher.

6.4 Hotels are assigned from Contoso Air contracted hotels. Passengers cannot choose a specific hotel or room type.

6.5 Customer Care cannot promise a hotel before eligibility is confirmed under Sections 3 and 5.

6.6 When contracted hotels near the airport are fully booked, airport customer service or the IROPS Recovery Desk will advise the passenger. Passengers who then book their own accommodation may request reimbursement under Section 10, subject to the caps in Section 10.3.

6.7 Hotel accommodation covers the room charge and taxes for one night. Incidental charges, such as room service, minibar, parking and telephone charges, are the passenger's responsibility.

## 7. Geographic Limitations

7.1 Hotel accommodation is not provided at the passenger's city of residence or home airport metropolitan area, as identified in the customer profile or by the reservation's origin when the trip begins at home. In that case, passengers are expected to return home.

7.2 For controllable overnight disruptions in the home airport metropolitan area, reasonable ground transportation home of up to US$50 per reservation may be reimbursed.

7.3 International stations follow applicable local regulations where they provide greater entitlements than this policy.

7.4 Example: a passenger whose trip began at home and whose outbound flight is cancelled for a controllable reason, with reaccommodation the next day, is not provided a hotel and may claim up to US$50 per reservation for transportation home. The same passenger disrupted overnight on the return journey at the destination city qualifies for hotel accommodation.

## 8. Exceptions

8.1 No meal or hotel assistance is provided for:

- voluntary (customer-requested) changes;
- missed connections caused by separate tickets;
- passengers who decline an offered same-day eligible alternative;
- delays of less than 3 hours; or
- uncontrollable disruptions, except the distressed passenger hotel rates in Section 3.2.

8.2 Customer Care cannot promise hotels before eligibility is confirmed.

8.3 Any exception to this policy must be approved by the Manager, Irregular Operations Customer Support, or the IROPS Recovery Desk duty manager, and recorded in the reservation notes.

## 9. How Vouchers Are Issued

9.1 Meal, hotel and ground transportation vouchers are issued by airport customer service or the IROPS Recovery Desk.

9.2 Vouchers are not issued by Tier 1 digital channels. Digital channels, including the Contoso Air digital assistant, may explain eligibility and direct passengers to airport customer service or the IROPS Recovery Desk at 1-800-555-0161.

9.3 Vouchers are issued electronically to the mobile number or email address on the reservation, or printed at the airport customer service counter.

9.4 Vouchers are issued only after the issuing team has confirmed the disruption reason code, the wait or overnight condition and the passenger's home airport metropolitan area.

## 10. Documentation Requirements and Reimbursement

10.1 When vouchers are unavailable, passengers may request reimbursement of eligible expenses.

10.2 Reimbursement requires itemized receipts submitted within 30 days of the disruption through the Contoso Air customer relations form.

10.3 Reimbursement caps are:

- US$40 per passenger per day for meals;
- US$150 per room per night for hotel; and
- US$50 per reservation for ground transportation home under Section 7.2.

10.4 Alcohol is not reimbursed.

10.5 Reimbursement is issued within 4 to 6 weeks after a complete request is received.

10.6 The request must include the confirmation code, the names of the passengers and the itemized receipts. Card statements alone are not accepted.

10.7 Approved reimbursements are paid to the card or bank account specified in the request. Requests submitted more than 30 days after the disruption, or without itemized receipts, are declined.

## 11. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 Customer Care | Explain eligibility and direct passengers to the issuing teams. |
| Airport customer service | Confirm eligibility and issue vouchers. |
| IROPS Recovery Desk | Confirm eligibility, issue vouchers remotely and approve exceptions. |
| Customer Relations | Review and pay reimbursement requests. |
| Manager, Irregular Operations Customer Support | Own this policy and approve exceptions. |
