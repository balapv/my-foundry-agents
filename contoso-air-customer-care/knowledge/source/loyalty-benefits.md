## 1. Purpose

1.1 This document defines the membership tiers of Contoso Air Altitude, the qualification requirement for each tier, the benefits available at each tier and the terms that govern their use. It is the authoritative reference for Customer Care when answering member questions about status and benefits.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This document applies to all Contoso Air Altitude members and to all Customer Care channels, including Tier 1 Customer Care (frontline agents and the Contoso Air digital assistant), Tier 2 Customer Care Specialists and Supervisors, the IROPS Recovery Desk and airport customer service.

## 3. Definitions

3.1 Member: an individual enrolled in Contoso Air Altitude.

3.2 Qualifying miles: miles flown on paid tickets for Contoso Air-operated flights that count toward tier status. Award travel, purchased miles and miles earned with non-airline partners are not qualifying miles.

3.3 Membership year: the calendar year, from January 1 to December 31.

3.4 Companion: a passenger traveling with the member on the same reservation (confirmation code) and on the same Contoso Air-operated flights.

3.5 Qualifying disruption: a carrier-initiated cancellation (a Contoso Air flight cancelled by Contoso Air for any reason, whether controllable or uncontrollable) or a significant delay (a departure delay of 3 hours or more versus scheduled departure), as defined in the Flight Disruption and Rebooking Policy (CA-CC-POL-014).

3.6 Space-available: provided only if a seat remains unsold and unassigned when the request is processed. Space-available benefits are never guaranteed.

## 4. Tiers and Qualification

4.1 Contoso Air Altitude has four tiers. Status is based on qualifying miles earned in a calendar year.

| Tier | Qualifying miles per calendar year | Miles earned per dollar |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 0 (on enrollment) | 5 |
| Silver | 25,000 | 7 |
| Gold | 50,000 | 8 |
| Platinum | 75,000 | 11 |

4.2 A member who reaches a tier threshold holds that tier from the date of qualification through December 31 of the following calendar year.

4.3 Qualifying miles reset to zero on January 1 each year and do not roll over.

4.4 Customer Care cannot change a member's tier, add qualifying miles or extend status. Members may request credit for a missing flight through their Altitude account up to 6 months after the flight date.

## 5. Benefits by Tier

### 5.1 Basic

- Earn 5 miles per dollar.
- Standard boarding.
- Online check-in.
- No fee waivers. Standard fees apply to checked bags, seat selection and same-day changes.

### 5.2 Silver

- Earn 7 miles per dollar.
- One free checked bag for the member.
- Group 3 boarding.
- Complimentary Economy Plus seat at check-in, subject to availability, for the member only.
- Space-available upgrades at the gate only.

### 5.3 Gold

- Earn 8 miles per dollar.
- Two free checked bags for the member and up to 1 companion on the same reservation.
- Group 2 boarding.
- Complimentary Economy Plus seat assignment at booking for the member and 1 companion.
- Complimentary upgrade requests processed 72 hours before departure, space-available, for the member and 1 companion.
- Waived same-day standby fee.
- Dedicated Gold service line: 1-800-555-0157.
- Priority reaccommodation during qualifying disruptions, as described in Section 6.
- No lounge access. Gold members may purchase lounge day passes at the member rate.

### 5.4 Platinum

- Earn 11 miles per dollar.
- Three free checked bags for the member and up to 8 companions on the same reservation.
- Group 1 boarding.
- Complimentary Economy Plus seat assignment at booking for the member and up to 8 companions on the same reservation.
- Complimentary upgrade requests processed 120 hours before departure, space-available, for the member and 1 companion.
- Same-day confirmed changes at no fee.
- Four lounge day passes per membership year.
- Highest priority reaccommodation during qualifying disruptions, as described in Section 6.
- Dedicated Platinum service line: 1-800-555-0158.

## 6. Priority Reaccommodation During Qualifying Disruptions

6.1 During a carrier-initiated cancellation or significant delay, Gold members and companions on the same reservation are placed ahead of non-elite customers (Basic members and customers who are not Altitude members) and Silver members in the reaccommodation queue.

6.2 Platinum members and companions on the same reservation receive the highest priority and are placed ahead of Gold members.

6.3 Priority reaccommodation does not guarantee seats, does not create additional inventory, does not displace confirmed passengers, and does not provide compensation, cash, upgrades or lounge access.

6.4 Reaccommodation itself follows CA-CC-POL-014, including the definition of an eligible alternative, the rules for keeping a traveling party together and the rules for cabin handling.

6.5 Priority reaccommodation does not apply to customer-requested changes.

6.6 Example: when a flight is cancelled and an eligible alternative has a limited number of seats, the reaccommodation system processes Platinum reservations first, then Gold reservations, then all other reservations. A Gold member traveling with two companions on one reservation is processed as one traveling party. If the eligible alternative cannot seat the whole party, the party is not split without the explicit consent of the primary traveler, and the rules in CA-CC-POL-014 Section 9 apply.

6.7 Customer Care must not describe priority reaccommodation as a guarantee of a seat on any specific flight.

## 7. Upgrades

7.1 Complimentary upgrades are to the next higher cabin and are always space-available. Requests are processed by tier and then by the time the request was made.

7.2 Basic fares are not eligible for complimentary upgrades.

7.3 A confirmed complimentary upgrade does not transfer automatically when a flight is disrupted. The request is reprocessed on the new flight, subject to availability.

7.4 Complimentary upgrades are never provided as compensation for disruptions. Placement in a higher cabin during reaccommodation under CA-CC-POL-014 is not an upgrade benefit.

## 8. Baggage, Seating and Boarding

8.1 Free checked bags apply to standard bags within published size and weight limits. Overweight and oversize fees still apply.

8.2 Companion benefits apply only when the companion is on the same reservation and the same flights as the member.

8.3 Complimentary Economy Plus seats are subject to availability. No compensation is provided when an Economy Plus seat is not available.

8.4 The member's boarding group applies to companions on the same reservation.

## 9. Same-Day Changes and Lounge Access

9.1 Same-day changes are customer-requested changes and must be for the same origin and destination on the same day. Basic fares are not eligible.

9.2 Gold members receive a waiver of the same-day standby fee for the member and 1 companion on the same reservation. The same-day confirmed change fee of US$75 per passenger applies to Gold members.

9.3 Platinum members may make same-day confirmed changes at no fee for the member and companions on the same reservation.

9.4 Platinum lounge day passes are deposited in the member account at the start of each membership year, or on qualification. Each pass admits one person for one day; unused passes expire on December 31.

9.5 Gold members have no lounge access but may purchase day passes at the member rate. Basic and Silver members have no lounge access benefit.

## 10. General Terms

10.1 Benefits apply to Contoso Air-operated flights, are personal to the member, and cannot be sold, transferred, combined to create new benefits, or redeemed for cash.

10.2 Status does not entitle members to compensation beyond published policies, and Customer Care may not grant benefits beyond the member's tier.

10.3 Complimentary upgrades are never provided as compensation for disruptions.

## 11. Customer Care Handling

11.1 Customer Care must confirm the member's current tier in the member profile before explaining or applying a benefit, and must verify identity under the Customer Authentication Standard (CA-CC-STD-002).

11.2 Customer Care must describe benefits exactly as set out in this document. Requests for benefits, compensation or exceptions beyond the member's tier must be handled under the Customer Service Escalation Guide (CA-CC-GDE-007).

11.3 Members may contact Customer Care at 1-800-555-0142, the Gold service line at 1-800-555-0157 or the Platinum service line at 1-800-555-0158.

11.4 When a member asks for a benefit that belongs to a higher tier, Customer Care must explain which tier provides the benefit and must not apply it. For example, a Silver member is not entitled to an Economy Plus seat at booking, and a Gold member is not entitled to lounge day passes without purchase.

11.5 When a member's flight is disrupted, Customer Care must explain that status provides priority in the reaccommodation queue only, and that meal, hotel, refund and rebooking entitlements are the same for all passengers under CA-CC-POL-014 and CA-CC-POL-021.

## 12. Exceptions

12.1 Exceptions may be approved only by the Senior Manager, Loyalty Programs, and must be recorded in the member profile. Tier 1 and Tier 2 Customer Care cannot grant exceptions to this document.
