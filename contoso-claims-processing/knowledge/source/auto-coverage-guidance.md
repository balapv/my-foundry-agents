## 1. Purpose

This guidance explains how claims staff apply the coverages of the Contoso Insurance Personal Auto Policy to physical damage claims. It is a practical summary for claim handling. The policy contract, endorsements and applicable state law control. When this guidance does not clearly answer a coverage question, the question is referred for coverage review (Section 11) rather than answered by assumption.

Coverage positions are communicated to customers only by authorized claims staff, after the policy record has been reviewed.

## 2. Reading the policy record

The policy record in the claims system is the authoritative source for coverage. For each claim, staff review:

| Policy record element | What to confirm |
|---|---|
| Policy status | Whether the policy is Active, Expired or Cancelled |
| Term effective and expiration dates | Whether the date of loss falls within the term |
| Cancellation details | Whether a cancellation took effect before the date of loss |
| Listed vehicles and their added and removed dates | Whether the vehicle involved was on the policy on the date of loss |
| Vehicle coverages | Which physical damage coverages apply to that vehicle |
| Deductibles | The deductible for the applicable coverage |
| Policy-level coverages | Liability and injury coverages that apply to the policy as a whole |
| Loss payee | Any lender or lessor that must be named on physical damage payments |

The current status of a policy is not the same as its status on the date of loss. A policy that is active today may not have been in force on an earlier date of loss, and a policy that has since expired may have been in force when the loss occurred. Coverage is always assessed as of the date of loss.

## 3. Active-policy requirement

Physical damage coverage applies only to a loss that occurs while the policy is in force for the vehicle involved. All of the following must be true:

- the date of loss is on or after the term effective date and before the term expiration date
- no cancellation took effect on or before the date of loss
- the vehicle was listed on the policy on the date of loss (on or after its added date, and before any removed date)
- the applicable coverage was listed for that vehicle

If any of these conditions is not met, or cannot be confirmed from the records, coverage is not confirmed. The adjuster records the specific condition that is not met and refers the claim for coverage review. Staff do not tell a customer that a claim is denied because of a date or status issue; coverage determinations of that kind are made through the coverage review process, which considers reinstatement, grace periods, renewal processing and data-entry corrections before any position is taken.

## 4. Collision coverage

Collision coverage pays for direct and accidental physical damage to the covered vehicle caused by:

- the covered vehicle colliding with another vehicle or object, or
- the covered vehicle overturning.

Collision coverage applies regardless of who was at fault. When another driver is responsible, Contoso Insurance may pay the collision claim under the customer's policy and then seek recovery from the responsible party or their insurer (subrogation).

Covered collision damage is damage caused by the collision itself. It includes damage directly caused by the impact and damage that results directly from that impact, such as a component displaced or broken by the force of the collision. It does not include damage that existed before the collision or damage from an unrelated event (Section 8).

## 5. Comprehensive coverage

Comprehensive (other than collision) coverage pays for direct and accidental physical damage to the covered vehicle that is not caused by collision or overturn. Common comprehensive losses include:

- theft of the vehicle or its parts
- vandalism and malicious mischief
- glass breakage
- fire, hail, windstorm, flood and falling objects
- contact with a bird or animal

A comprehensive claim requires that comprehensive coverage was listed for the vehicle on the date of loss. A customer who has collision coverage but not comprehensive coverage does not have physical damage coverage for a comprehensive loss, and the reverse is also true.

## 6. Liability coverage overview

Bodily injury liability and property damage liability coverages pay for injury to other people and damage to other people's property for which the insured is legally responsible, up to the policy limits. Property damage liability pays for damage to the other party's vehicle or property. It does not pay for damage to the insured's own vehicle; that damage is handled under collision or comprehensive coverage.

Liability determinations depend on the facts of the incident, including the police report, statements and applicable law. Claims staff do not state a liability position before the liability review is complete.

## 7. Deductibles

### 7.1 How deductibles apply

The deductible is the portion of a covered physical damage loss that the customer pays. It is set separately for each coverage and each vehicle and is shown in the policy record. When a covered loss is settled:

- the deductible for the applicable coverage is subtracted from the covered amount of the loss
- one deductible applies per covered occurrence, per vehicle
- the deductible applies to collision and comprehensive coverage; it does not apply to liability coverage
- when the covered amount is less than the deductible, no physical damage payment is made under that coverage

The deductible shown on the policy record for the vehicle and coverage in force on the date of loss is the deductible that applies. Deductibles are not changed after a loss has occurred.

### 7.2 Deductibles and subrogation

When another party is responsible for a collision, the collision deductible still applies when Contoso Insurance pays the claim under the customer's collision coverage. If Contoso Insurance later recovers from the responsible party, the customer's deductible may be reimbursed in whole or in part from the recovery, as described in the policy contract. Staff do not promise a deductible reimbursement before a recovery is made.

### 7.3 Stating the deductible

When summarizing coverage for a claim, state the coverage that applies and the deductible amount from the policy record. The deductible is not an estimate of the final payment; the final payment depends on the covered amount of the loss after review.

## 8. Pre-existing and unrelated damage

Physical damage coverage applies to damage caused by the covered loss. It does not apply to:

- damage that existed before the date of loss (pre-existing damage)
- damage caused by a different, earlier or later event (unrelated damage)
- wear and tear, deterioration, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or road damage to tires, unless caused by a covered loss

When a repair is requested for damage that may be pre-existing or unrelated, the adjuster does not decide the question from assumption. The adjuster compares the requested repair with the evidence of the reported loss, considers the customer's prior claim history for the same area of the vehicle where relevant, and requests the information needed to determine whether the damage was caused by the covered loss. Damage that may be unrelated is not covered under the current claim unless the evidence shows that the current loss caused it, but it may be covered under a separate claim if it resulted from another covered event.

A request to repair an area that the evidence does not currently connect to the reported loss is a question of evidence. It is reviewed as described in the Repair Estimate Review Guidelines.

## 9. Customer-requested upgrades and betterment

Physical damage coverage restores the vehicle to its condition before the loss. It does not pay for:

- upgrades requested by the customer (for example, a higher trim component, an accessory or a cosmetic change)
- repairs to undamaged parts to match a customer preference
- betterment, where a replacement part materially improves the vehicle beyond its pre-loss condition

When an estimate includes a customer-requested upgrade, the covered amount is limited to the cost of restoring the pre-loss condition. The customer may pay the difference directly to the repair facility.

## 10. Other exclusions relevant to physical damage claims

The following exclusions frequently arise in physical damage claims. Refer to the policy contract for the complete wording:

- intentional damage caused by or at the direction of an insured
- use of the vehicle in a race or speed contest
- use of the vehicle to carry people or property for a fee, unless the policy is endorsed for that use
- loss to a vehicle not listed on the policy, unless it qualifies as a newly acquired or temporary substitute vehicle under the policy terms
- custom equipment beyond the amount shown on the policy

The presence of an exclusion is determined from the facts. An exclusion is not applied on suspicion alone.

## 11. Coverage uncertainty and escalation

Coverage uncertainty exists when the records do not clearly show whether coverage applied on the date of loss, or when the facts may fall within an exclusion. Examples include:

- the date of loss is near the start or end of the policy term or a cancellation date
- dated documents give different dates of loss
- the vehicle involved does not match a vehicle listed on the policy for that date
- the facts suggest a possible exclusion
- the customer disputes the coverage shown on the policy record

When coverage is uncertain, the adjuster:

1. Records the specific question and the records reviewed.
2. Does not communicate a coverage position to the customer.
3. Refers the question to a claims supervisor or the coverage review team.
4. Continues gathering the evidence needed to resolve the question.

Only an authorized coverage reviewer or claims supervisor may issue a coverage denial or reservation of rights. Decision-support tools may identify a coverage question; they do not resolve it.

## 12. Summary table

| Situation | Coverage treatment | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Collision with another vehicle; policy active; collision coverage listed for the vehicle | Collision coverage applies to damage caused by the collision, less the collision deductible | Continue normal claim review |
| Date of loss after term expiration or after a cancellation took effect | Coverage not confirmed | Coverage review; no coverage position to the customer until complete |
| Vehicle not listed on the policy on the date of loss | Coverage not confirmed | Coverage review |
| Comprehensive loss; comprehensive coverage not listed | No physical damage coverage for this loss under the policy record | Coverage review before any communication |
| Repair requested for an area the evidence does not connect to the loss | Not confirmed as covered damage for this claim | Request supporting evidence under estimate review |
| Customer-requested upgrade on the estimate | Covered only to pre-loss condition | Adjust covered amount after review |
